# HealthCare-Patient-Analytics (Power BI Dashboard Project)

## Project Objective
To analyze patient admission records, discharge timelines, billing vs insurance trends, and doctor feedback. The goal is to derive actionable insights that support hospital management decisions using Power BI.

---

## Dataset Used

- <a href="https://github.com/vinayakgupta781/HealthCare-Patient-Analytics-Dashboard-Power-BI-Project-/blob/main/HealthCare%20Project.pbix">Dataset</a>

---

##  Questions (KPIs)

- What is the bed occupancy distribution (Private, General, ICU)?
- What are the most common diagnosis types among patients?
- Which doctor received the most feedback and patient volume?
- What is the trend of billing amount vs health insurance claims?
- Which patient records are tied to the highest/lowest billings?
- What is the billing pattern across different diagnosis types?

## Process

- Cleaned and transformed dataset in Power BI
- Ensured proper data types (Date, Currency)
- Created measures for billing totals, insurance comparisons
- Designed visual reports using charts, slicers & filters
- Applied color themes and formatted dashboards for clarity

## Project Insights

- Private Beds were the most occupied
- Viral Infections were the most common diagnosis
- All doctors received equal feedback volume (in this sample)
- Billing Amounts are generally higher than Insurance Claimed
- Highest expenses were for Viral Infection and Flu

---

## Final Conclusion

This Power BI dashboard provides valuable operational insights for hospital administrators:
- Better understanding of billing and insurance gaps
- Identifying most common diagnoses to plan resources
- Tracking doctor performance and workload
- Managing bed type demand more efficiently
